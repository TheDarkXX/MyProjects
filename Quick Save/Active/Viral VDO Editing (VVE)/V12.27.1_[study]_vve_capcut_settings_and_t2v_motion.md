# 🎛️ CapCut Settings Decoded & T2V Motion Update

## 📌 Context (Compiled Truth)
- **T2V Motion Control**: We updated the `doctorbank_broll_text2video.yaml` to change "MOTION LOCK" to "MOTION CONTROL". Previously, the Medical category forced a strict `ZERO zoom, ZERO pan` lock. The new truth allows context-aware slow movements (e.g., `Extremely slow, smooth push-in` or `Slow 360-degree orbit`) for organs, preventing AI hallucinations while keeping shots cinematic.
- **CapCut Subtitle Position (Y=-700)**: CapCut does not store subtitle coordinates in exact pixels. A Y-position of `-700` in the UI for a 1080x1920 video is stored in `draft_content.json` as a ratio: `-0.3645833333333333` (because 1920 * -0.364583... = -700.0).
- **CapCut Enhance Voice**: The "Enhance Voice" toggle is not stored in general audio settings. It creates a dedicated object in `materials.vocal_beautifys` with properties like `ambient_sound_level: 25` and maps strictly to time ranges on the track.
- **Perfect Mark**: We used `mark_perfect.py` (Perfect Mark #2) to snapshot the exact manual refinements on the Test Auto timeline.
