# Fasting Timer V1.1.0: Smart Gap Decision Gate & Continuous Timeline Architecture

## 📌 Context & Implementation (Compiled Truth)
- **Problem Statement:**
  When a user forgets to toggle the fasting timer or returns after multiple days of off-track eating, previous rigid auto-stitch algorithms created artificial 40h+ fasting records. Conversely, starting fresh without prompting wiped out legitimate forgotten fasts, skewing long-term autophagy compliance. Furthermore, client-to-server clock skew (+59.88s) triggered false future timestamp rejections (HTTP 400).

- **Architectural Solution & Decision Gate Matrix:**
  1. **Smart Gap Detection Threshold (4h):**
     - If elapsed eating duration or untracked gap <= 4 hours: System assumes natural daily variance and executes seamless switch instantly (0s gap).
     - If elapsed duration > 4 hours: Triggers #modalGapDecision bottom sheet modal with 3 distinct recovery pathways.

  | Recovery Option | User Scenario | Backend Behavior | Timeline Impact |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **Option 1: Continue Fast** | Forgotten toggle (true fast maintained) | Caps previous eating at reasonable target (1-2h), sets started_at_utc = prev.ended_at_utc | 100% continuous accumulated autophagy hours preserved |
  | **Option 2: Fresh Start** | Off-track eating / cheat period | Caps previous session with [break], starts fresh session at now with [fresh_start] note | Clean slate, gap displayed as Untracked Break |
  | **Option 3: Custom Time** | Known exact finish time (e.g., 2h ago) | Starts session and opens 24h virtual loop Drum Roller for backdated entry | Exact physiological timestamp entered by user |

- **Backend Safeguards & Intentional Break Guard:**
  - Updated realignTimelineAndRecalculate in routes/fasting.js with forceStitchAll = false.
  - Gaps exceeding 12 hours or tagged with [break]/[fresh_start] are preserved as intentional breaks and will NOT be auto-merged into multi-day fake fasts.
  - Widened future timestamp prevention tolerance from 60s to 15 minutes with smart auto-clamp to server now.

- **Visual Ledger & History Hygiene:**
  - Dynamically injects .untracked-gap-divider cards into History ledger rendering whenever adjacent sessions have > 4h gaps.
  - Ensures transparency and high-fidelity reporting of actual fasting habits.
