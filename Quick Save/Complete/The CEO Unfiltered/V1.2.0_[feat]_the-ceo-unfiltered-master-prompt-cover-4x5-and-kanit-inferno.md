# V1.2.0 [feat] The CEO Unfiltered: Master Prompt Formula 4:5, Kanit Typography Hierarchy & Raging Inferno Cover

## 📌 Context & Implementation (Compiled Truth)

### 1. Reverse-Engineered Master Prompt Formula (4:5 Pattern Interrupt)
- Codified the definitive 6-pillar framework in [`The CEO Unfiltered/MASTER_PROMPT_COVER_4X5.md`](file:///c:/My%20Claw/MyProjects/The%20CEO%20Unfiltered/MASTER_PROMPT_COVER_4X5.md):
  1. **Format & Lens:** 4:5 Vertical (1080x1350 / 1122x1402) with close-up macro framing and top breathing room.
  2. **Hero Conflict:** Tangible, revered self-help objects undergoing aggressive, photorealistic destruction.
  3. **Authentic Material DNA:** Explicitly banned generic antique leather encyclopedias; enforced modern paperback textures with authentic publisher visual elements.
  4. **Atmosphere & Props:** Dark CEO executive suite, green digital clock showing `05:00 AM`, sunrise skyline contrast, and metallic bull decor.
  5. **Kanit Typography Hierarchy:** Explicitly locked `Kanit ExtraBold (800)` for red attention box, `Kanit Bold (700)` for top hook/badges, and `Kanit Medium (500)` for subheadline capsule.
  6. **Negative Constraints:** Banned fantasy/cartoon elements, removed EP numbering on master images, prevented clutter.

### 2. Sourced 5 Authentic Thai Edition Book Covers
- Crawled and saved high-resolution, authentic Thai cover images from bookstore repositories into `The CEO Unfiltered/assets/real_books/`:
  - `atomic_habits.jpg` (WeLearn / Change+ — white cover, gold title, tiny dots pattern)
  - `deep_work.jpg` (WeLearn — concentric arch graphic in blue, Cal Newport)
  - `miracle_morning.jpg` (Bee Media — rising sun red horizon graphic)
  - `psychology_of_money.jpg` (Live Rich — folded-money brain graphic)
  - `thinking_fast_slow.jpg` (WeLearn — thick white spine, yellow sharpened pencil graphic)

### 3. Kanit Font Hierarchy Integration & Script Upgrade
- Downloaded [`Kanit-ExtraBold.ttf`](file:///c:/My%20Claw/MyProjects/The%20CEO%20Unfiltered/fonts/Kanit-ExtraBold.ttf) (Weight 800) into `fonts/`.
- Updated `scripts/render_pattern_interrupt_cover.py`:
  - Registered `@font-face` across 4 weights: ExtraBold (800), Bold (700), SemiBold (600), Medium (500).
  - Wired `.line1` to Kanit Bold (700), `.line2` (red box) to Kanit ExtraBold (800), and `.sub-pill` to Kanit Medium (500).
  - Added dynamic canvas width/height parameters (1080x1350 for 4:5 vertical) and conditional episode tag rendering.

### 4. S-Tier Raging Inferno Master Cover Generation
- Discovered and fixed the "tame fire" issue: Promoted fire from mild top burning to a violent, high-dissonance raging inferno with charred edges, collapsed stack physics, flying embers, and dark smoke.
- Relocated `AI ตบกะโหลก!` badge from top-right to bottom-right:
  - Clears upper visual clutter, giving 100% focus to the red hero headline box.
  - Establishes a balanced dual-signature frame at the base: `THE CEO UNFILTERED` (bottom-left) x `AI ตบกะโหลก!` (bottom-right).
- Saved as the project's absolute master asset: [`The CEO Unfiltered/assets/EP01_cover_burn_inferno_4x5_master.png`](file:///c:/My%20Claw/MyProjects/The%20CEO%20Unfiltered/assets/EP01_cover_burn_inferno_4x5_master.png) (and mirrored as `EP01_cover_burn_books_4x5_master.png`).
