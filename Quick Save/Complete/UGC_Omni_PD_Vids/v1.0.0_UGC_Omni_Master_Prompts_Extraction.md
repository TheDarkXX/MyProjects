### 1. Reverse Engineering `PD App.exe` (v2.1.33)
- Unpacked `resources/app.asar` and deobfuscated `javascript-obfuscator` string-table calls (`_0x...`) across `main.js` (20,059 strings), `ui/ui.js` (44,993 strings), `ui/vids-lab.js` (739 strings), and `ui/autopilot.js`.
- **Architecture Discovery**: PD App uses a 2-tier prompt system:
  1. Server-side gateway (`/v1/pd-build` on `pd-auto-ai-gateway.filmbancha127.workers.dev`) requiring `pdvip_` / `PDVL-` key to generate per-scene JSON (`vids_scenes`, max 600 chars/scene).
  2. Client-side Master Prompts & Guard Locks embedded locally in `main.js` and `ui/ui.js`:
     - **Product Name Sanitizer**: Strips brackets, SKUs, units, and promo words down to 3–4 natural words so the AI voiceover speaks naturally.
     - **Reference Image & Anti-Static-Frame Lock**: Forces the video model to redraw character and product rather than pasting the attached static image as the opening frame.
     - **Anti-Morphing Product Lock**: Prohibits changing packaging (e.g., bottle to pouch), adding unrequested unboxing scenes, or hallucinating logos.
     - **Apparel / Model Separation Lock (`APPAREL_INSTR`)**: Designates the last attached image as the model's face/body and earlier images as the exact product/outfit.
     - **Multi-Scene Extend Continuity Lock**: Prepends strict continuation instructions on Scene 2+ so Extend clips do not restart hooks or repeat greetings.
- **Viable Options Extracted**:
  - `vlStruct` / `ftStyle`: 18 story structures (`problem_solution`, `hard_sell`, `storytelling`, `lifestyle`, `ugc_review`, `pai_ya`, `before_after`, `asmr_satisfying`, `warehouse_hands`, etc.).
  - `vlLook` / `CLIP_STYLES`: 11 categories, 86 scene/visual styles + 5 Vids Lab favorites (`review`, `live`, `lifestyle`, `studio`, `random`).
  - `vlCoverStyle`: 5 categories, 28 cover thumbnail styles (`real_home`, `model_hero`, `model_ugc`, `text_giant`, `explode_3d`, etc.).
