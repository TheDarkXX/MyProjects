# VVE Masterprompt & Dopamine Rules (Scene Generator)

## 📌 Context (Compiled Truth)
- Designed the "Image & Motion Masterprompt" architecture for VVE Step 5 (Scene Generator), specifically tailored for the `DoctorBank` health/medical channel.
- Expanded the prompt scope to encompass 8 distinct categories (`A_People`, `B_Organs`, `C_Micro`, `D_Metaphor`, `E_Food`, `F_Product`, `G_Lifestyle`, `H_Nature`) to thoroughly cover all types of B-Roll used in health-related short-form videos (TikTok/Reels).
- Established the **Hybrid Strategy**: 
  - For symptoms/daily life (Relatable topics), strictly enforce **Premium UGC** styles featuring well-groomed Thai women (aged 40-60) in **Tight Close-up (CU) or Head-and-shoulders MCU**. This actively prevents the AI from generating wide shots or full-body shots when describing people sitting in rooms or on beds.
  - For scientific/mechanism topics, use **Microscopic Medical CG** or **Visual Metaphors** (e.g., rusted joints for knee osteoarthritis).
- Refined the **Dopamine Hit Rule**: Instead of forcing a static rotation pattern (like A->C->E->A), the rule mandates that the **subtitle content strictly dictates the category**, but the AI must alter the camera angle or composition every 3-5 seconds to maintain visual engagement, even if the category repeats.
- Abstracted the prompt configuration out of `doctorbank.yaml` into a dedicated `doctorbank_masterprompt.yaml` for cleaner architecture.
