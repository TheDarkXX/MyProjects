# [Fix] X-Chart Watchlist: TO THE MOON Holding Badge & No Chase Isolation

## 📌 Context & Implementation (Compiled Truth)
- **Problem Statement**:
  - In X-Chart Watchlist & Holdings, portfolio assets triggering Scenario 14 (e.g. CRWD in Doctorbank Growth holding) were displaying `⛔` and labeled as `MOON (No Chase) ยอดดอยห้ามไล่ราคา` instead of `🚀 TO THE MOON (รันเทรนด์ปล่อยกำไรวิ่ง)`.
- **Root Cause**:
  - In [`TierBadgeIndicator.tsx`](file:///c:/My%20Claw/MyProjects/My%20Stock%20Portfolio/src/components/xchart/TierBadgeIndicator.tsx#L94), `isNoChaseBadge` checked `upperBadge.includes('MOON') && !upperBadge.includes('MOONSHOT')`.
  - When a held asset triggered S14 with `badge: 'To The Moon'`, `upperBadge.includes('MOON')` was true, turning it into No Chase even though `traffic_light === 'TO_THE_MOON'` indicated an active position to hold and ride.
- **Architectural Solution & Decision**:
  - Evaluated alternative metaphors (🌕 Full Moon vs 🚀 Rocket). Retained `🚀` (Rocket) as confirmed by user for maximum clarity and momentum alignment.
  - Refactored `isNoChaseBadge` to strictly check `upperBadge.includes('NO CHASE') || upperBadge.includes('⛔')`.
  - Added `!isRunnerBadge` guard to prevent S15 `RUNNER` badges from being intercepted by the `TO_THE_MOON` block.
  - Verified with `npx tsc --noEmit` and bumped cache (`npm run bump-cache`).
