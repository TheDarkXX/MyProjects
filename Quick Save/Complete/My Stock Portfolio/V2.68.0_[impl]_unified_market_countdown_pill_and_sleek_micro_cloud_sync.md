# V2.68.0: Unified Market Countdown Pill & Sleek Micro Cloud Sync

## 📌 Context & Implementation (Compiled Truth)
- **Problem & Redundancy Purge**: Previously, the header tab bar featured disconnected `CLOSED` / `US LIVE`, `⚡ Price Feed`, and `🟡 Synced · just now` badges. This created unnecessary visual noise and confused settings synchronization with stock price updates.
- **Unified Market Status Hook (`useMarketStatus.ts`)**: Built a unified real-time hook referencing `America/New_York` timezone:
  - `● LIVE`: Active during regular trading hours (09:30 - 16:00 ET) with emerald pulse dot.
  - `● OPENS IN [1h 24m / 45m]`: Dynamic countdown window activated 120 minutes prior to market bell. Initiates subtle breathing pulse in the final 30 minutes.
  - `● CLOSED · [Date / Holiday]`: Accurately displays the previous session's official close date or holiday name without erroneous relative "yesterday" labels.
  - **Holiday & DST Engine**: Integrated full 2025-2027 US market holiday registry (`US_HOLIDAYS_MAP`), early close schedule (13:00 ET on Black Friday/Christmas Eve), and automatic backward day skipping.
- **Relocated Universal Cloud Sync (`CloudSyncBadge.tsx`)**: Upgraded with a minimalist `variant="icon"` (32x32px curved button) placed at the far right navigation dock, keeping background plumbing out of financial data capsules.
