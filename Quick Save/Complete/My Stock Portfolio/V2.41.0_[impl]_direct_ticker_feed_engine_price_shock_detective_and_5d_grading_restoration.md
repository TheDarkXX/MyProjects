# V2.41.0: Direct Ticker Ingestion Engine, Price Shock Detective, Macro Shield & 5D Scoring Restoration

## 📌 Context & Implementation (Compiled Truth)

### 1. The Core Architecture Challenge
Previously, the News Intelligence radar was tightly coupled to Beehiiv newsletter scrapers. If Beehiiv failed to publish on a given day or focused on non-portfolio topics, the entire dashboard was blind ("หน้าเว็บโล่ง"). Concurrently, when scraping high-volume tickers (like NVDA with 250+ articles), the system suffered from signal-to-noise degradation.

### 2. The Architectural Solution
We designed and shipped a **Two-Engine Hybrid Architecture**:
- **Engine A (Direct Ticker & Catalyst Shield):**
  - **23 Tracked Tickers:** 
    - Active Holdings: `NVDA`, `CRWD`, `RBRK`, `HIMS`, `MELI`, `META`, `SCHG`, `ISRG`, `AMZN`, `COST`, `ASTS`, `CRWV`, `RKLB`
    - Project 2X Targets (Both Portfolios): `TSM`, `AVGO`, `VRT`, `APH`, `KLAC`, `ANET`, `STRL`, `ALAB`, `PLTR`, `CLS`, `QQQM`
    - Dynamic Watchlist from SQLite
  - **Multi-Source Tier-1 Feeds:** Finnhub Company News API, Yahoo Finance Search, and Google News RSS.
  - **Strict Anti-Junk Filter:** 100% blacklist enforcement blocking Motley Fool, Zacks, 24/7 Wall St, TipRanks, and Stocktwits.
  - **Price Shock Detective:** Real-time quote polling via Yahoo Finance. Triggers targeted catalyst investigation if a Large Cap swings $\ge \pm 3.5\%$ or Growth/Mid Cap swings $\ge \pm 4.5\%$.
  - **Macro Shield:** Curates 1–2 critical macro items daily covering Fed interest rates, FOMC, and CPI/PCE inflation strictly from Reuters, Bloomberg, and CNBC, translating them into direct portfolio impact for growth tech.
- **Engine B (Beehiiv Scraper & Community Consensus):**
  - Preserved as supplementary multi-source consensus layer.

### 3. Critical Bug Discovery & Elimination: The "THE_MUST" Override
During production validation, the user observed that nearly all newly fetched news items displayed the red `🔥 THE_MUST` badge on the web interface. 
Investigation revealed a blunt hardcode in `server/services/newsRadar.js`:
```javascript
// Culprit line:
cand.shockInfo?.isShock ? 'THE_MUST' : (isMacro ? 'THE_MUST' : aiResult.reading_priority)
```
This forced any ticker that underwent a price shock (e.g., META +11%, CRWD +5%, ALAB +12%) or any Macro article to be stamped `'THE_MUST'`, completely ignoring the AI's 5D Content-Driven evaluation score (even items with scores of 5 to 25 points).

**The Solution:**
- Replaced the hardcode with `aiResult.reading_priority || 'CHATTER'` and `aiResult.impact_level || 'routine'`.
- Retained the price shock information as an amber UI badge (`⚡ Shock +X%`) and `PRICE_SHOCK` tag in `triage_tags`.
- Upgraded `server/scripts/migrate_to_4_tiers.js` to automatically reclassify all database entries across 4 tiers based on strict 5D score thresholds.
- Ran the migration on both local and VPS SQLite databases, successfully restoring the elite 6 `THE_MUST` catalysts while categorizing market commentary and price shock chatter into `CHATTER`.

## 📜 2. Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

### ADR-01: Decoupling News Ingestion from Beehiiv to Direct Ticker Feeds
* **Decision:** ปรับให้หุ้นในพอร์ต (Holdings) ดึงข่าวตรงจาก Finnhub + Yahoo Finance + Google Ticker RSS ได้ข่าวฉบับเต็มทันที 100%

### ADR-02: Strict Catalyst Guard & Blacklist Enforcement
* **Decision:** แบนเว็บขยะบล็อกเกอร์ (Motley Fool, Zacks, 24/7 Wall St, TipRanks) 100% และคัดกรองเฉพาะ Fundamental Catalysts

### ADR-03: Price Shock Detective
* **Decision:** ตรวจจับ Change% ของหุ้นในพอร์ต หากเหวี่ยงเกินเกณฑ์ ระบบจะยิงคำค้นหาเจาะจงสาเหตุและให้ AI อธิบายทันที

### ADR-04: Macro Shield & Portfolio Impact Translator
* **Decision:** ดักจับเฉพาะข่าว Fed/เงินเฟ้อ/ดอกเบี้ย จาก Tier-1 (Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC) วันละ 1-2 ข่าว พร้อมให้ AI แปลงเป็นผลกระทบตรงต่อพอร์ต Growth Tech
