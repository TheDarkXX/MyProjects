# Executive Investment Cockpit with Segmented Tabs & Ruthless Strategist AI System Prompt (V2.4.0)

## 💡 Context & Implementation (Compiled Truth)

### 1. Why this was done:
In the previous iteration (V2.3.0), the backend was successfully upgraded to fetch and parse 19 forward-looking Yahoo Finance metrics (Analyst Target Prices, Upside/Downside, Recommendation consensus, EPS growth forecast, Earnings beat streak) and generate a 15-section JSON analysis via GPT-5.6 Terra High in `strategist` mode.
However, two major architectural and UX problems emerged:
1. **Frontend "Long-Scroll Fatigue" & Data Blind Spots:** Displaying 15 comprehensive sections on a single continuous page required excessive vertical scrolling. Users were overwhelmed by walls of raw text and missed crucial data points like analyst consensus, forward EPS momentum, and stress test scenarios.
2. **AI Analysis Tone Was Generic and Weak:** Despite ordering a decisive "จอมมาร" (Ruthless) perspective, the existing system prompt designated the AI merely as a conventional Wall Street strategist with mild wording (`โทนเสียง: ตรงไปตรงมา ดุดัน กระชับ ฟันธง ไม่อ้อมค้อม (ไม่ใช้คำว่า มึง/กู)`). GPT-5.6 Terra defaulted to polite bank analyst jargon, failing to execute ruthless risk elimination, call out portfolio cancers, or order decisive capital redeployment.

### 2. Core Architecture & Implementations:

#### A. 2-Layer Executive Investment Cockpit Layout (`AIBlueprintAdvisor.tsx`):
The layout was completely redesigned into two distinct, intuitive layers:
- **Layer 1: Executive Cockpit (Pinned Top Summary):**
  - **Grade Card:** Giant letter display with glow effects color-coded to grade (A/B/C/D).
  - **5-Axis Health Radar:** Recharts-powered radar across Diversification, Valuation, Growth, Risk, and Income.
  - **4 Key Metric Badges:** Average Beta ($\beta$), Weighted P/E, Risk Score (/100), and Cash Allocation (%).
  - **Alert Banners:** Concentration Risk alert banner (orange/amber) and Dividend Health/Yield Trap banner (cyan/blue).
  - **Portfolio Style Pill:** Visual badge beneath the title categorizing portfolio personality.
- **Layer 2: 4 Segmented Tabs (`activeTab: 'plan' | 'stocks' | 'stress' | 'macro'`):**
  - **🎯 แผนปรับพอร์ต (Default Actionable Tab):** Before/After dual donut comparison, allocation delta table, timeline stepper, and suggestion cards.
  - **🔎 หุ้นรายตัว (Forward-Looking Stocks Tab):** Individual cards per stock integrating Yahoo consensus, upside badges, EPS growth, and beat streaks.
  - **🌪️ จำลองวิกฤต (Stress Test & Risk Tab):** Crisis simulation cards with drawdown severity meters, semi-circle risk gauge, and sector gap analysis.
  - **🌐 ภาพรวม (Macro & Qualitative Tab):** Macro economic outlook, strengths, structural weaknesses, and missing exposure badges.

#### B. 5 New Specialized Visual Sub-Components:
1. **`ConsensusBar.tsx` (`src/components/rebalance/advisor/`):**
   Stacked horizontal bar visualizing Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, and Sell counts from Wall Street consensus with proportional widths and tooltips.
2. **`StockVerdictCard.tsx` (`src/components/rebalance/advisor/`):**
   Full-featured card displaying ticker, grade, portfolio role, target price, dynamic green/red Upside/Downside badge, EPS next year growth pill, quarterly beat streak trophy, consensus bar, and expandable AI future outlook.
3. **`BeforeAfterDonut.tsx` (`src/components/rebalance/advisor/`):**
   Side-by-side SVG/Recharts Donut charts comparing current portfolio holding percentages vs ideal blueprint recommendations, highlighting newly recommended assets with glowing "✨ NEW" badges.
4. **`DrawdownMeter.tsx` (`src/components/rebalance/advisor/`):**
   Severity meter with gradient bar (green $\rightarrow$ yellow $\rightarrow$ orange $\rightarrow$ deep red) marking estimated crisis drawdown ranges (e.g. -20% to -35%).
5. **`TimelineStepper.tsx` (`src/components/rebalance/advisor/`):**
   Three-step horizontal timeline displaying Phase 1 (Immediate cuts, 1-2 weeks), Phase 2 (Mid-term reallocation, 1-3 months), and Phase 3 (Buffering/liquidity).

#### C. System Prompt Overhaul: "จอมมารแห่ง Wall Street" (`server/routes/ai-advisor.js`):
Rewrote the AI prompt to enforce the ruthless, uncompromising strategist persona while strictly eliminating profanity and "มึง/กู":

#### D. Force Cache Bypass Mechanism (`force: true`):
Previously, the backend cached analysis results for 6 hours based on portfolio ID and blueprint hash, which prevented users from seeing updated prompt results immediately.
- Added `force` parameter to `/api/ai-advisor` request payload.
- Backend skips SQLite cache lookup when `force === true`.
- Updated `api.ai.advisor(mode, blueprints, funData, portfolioId, force = false)` in `src/services/api.ts`.
- `AIBlueprintAdvisor.tsx` explicitly passes `true` upon manual button clicks.
