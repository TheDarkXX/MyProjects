### 2. State of Truth: BUY NOW Lifecycle & Bar-by-Bar Computation
- **No Fixed Expiration Timer**: The signal does not have a hardcoded expiration countdown. It is computed dynamically bar-by-bar on the daily candle (**Timeframe D1**) via [`project2xEngine.js`](file:///c:/My%20Claw/MyProjects/My%20Stock%20Portfolio/server/services/project2xEngine.js).
- **Practical Window (1 – 3 Trading Days)**:
  - BUY NOW is engineered as a **Sniper Entry Point**.
  - In *Scenario 8 (V-Shape Rebound)*, price must be within `[-3.0%, +2.5%]` of major EMAs. A successful bounce carries price > +2.5% away within 1-3 bars, naturally exiting the trigger zone.
  - In *Scenario 7 (Base Breakout)*, explosive volume ($volRatio \ge 1.4\times$) typically normalizes after 1-2 sessions.
  - In *Scenario 6 (Bear Trap Reclaim)*, the historical dip lookback is capped at 5 bars ($n-2$ to $n-6$).

### 3. State Transition Paths After BUY NOW
1. **Bullish Continuation ➔ `⚡ RUNNER` / `🚀 TO THE MOON`**:
   - Surfing above EMA 9 with institutional backing ($Banker \ge 10$, $d150 > 4\%$) transitions to `RUNNER` (Mandate: **HOLD / 0%**).
   - High altitude breakout ($d150 > +15\%$ Core, $+25\%$ Moonshot, $Banker \ge 12$) transitions to `TO THE MOON` (Holding: Let profits run; Unowned: `⛔ MOON No Chase`).
2. **Failed Rebound / Retest ➔ `⏳ GET READY`**:
   - Closing red (`isLatestBullish = false`) or dipping below EMA 9 (`isAboveEma9 = false`) reverts state to `GET READY` to prevent buying fakeouts.
3. **Severe Breakdown ➔ Layer 0 Veto**:
   - Structural collapse below EMA 200 or complete banker exit ($Banker = 0$) triggers `🔪 FALLING KNIFE` or `❌ MAYDAY EXIT`.

### 4. Critical Trading Prohibitions (Iron Rules)
- **❌ DO NOT Follow-Buy on `RUNNER ⚡`**:
  - Deploy tranche is strictly **0%**.
  - Stop loss is too far below current price; normal pullback tests (-3% to -5%) trigger panic stops.
  - Approaches the `MOON (No Chase)` ceiling.
  - Correct tactic: Wait for pullback to EMA 9 or EMA 50 to generate a fresh `GET READY` ➔ `BUY NOW` bounce.
- **❌ DO NOT Front-Run Accumulation in `GET READY ⏳`**:
  - Deploy tranche is strictly **0% (Standby / Prepare Cash)**.
  - Price remains submerged under EMA 9 or closed on a red candle. Buying early is catching a falling knife.
  - Full size (75% - 100%) is only deployed once green confirmation closes above EMA 9.

### 5. 3 Anti-FOMO Execution Protocols
1. **☕ 10-Second Morning Coffee Routine**: US markets close 03:00-04:00 Thai time. Review X-Chart at 07:00-08:00 using the `BUY NOW 🔥` quick-filter chip. Set Pre-market / MOO orders with brokers throughout the day.
2. **⚙️ Pre-emptive Buy Stop Orders**: When a stock is in `GET READY`, locate the exact EMA 9 price level on the HUD and place an automated Buy Stop order with the broker slightly above the trigger.
3. **📱 Price Alerts**: Set alerts on EMA 9 in TradingView or broker apps.
