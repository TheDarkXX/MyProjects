  - `upgradeDowngradeHistory`: Transaction event log capped at ~1,000 events (4-6 years for mega caps). Price targets are raw unadjusted nominal figures requiring cumulative split adjustment.
- **Quantitative Simulation Engine (`scratch/test_target_dca_backtest.py`):**
  - Reconstructed 1-year rolling analyst consensus mean (split-adjusted) from 2021 to 2026 (1,445 trading days, 70 monthly DCA cycles, $70,000 total capital).
  - Evaluated 3 strategies:

    2. **Hard-Cap DCA (Paused stock reallocated):** $326,977 (+367.11%, Max DD -35.71%). Minimal +0.24% alpha, 1.06% DD reduction.
    3. **Upside-Weighted DCA:** $324,539 (+363.63%, Max DD -35.42%). Negative alpha (-3.24%) due to starving super-performers (APH, ANET) where Wall Street targets lagged explosive growth, over-allocating into TSM ($103k vs $54k).
